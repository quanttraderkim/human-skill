# Human conversation comparison follow-up — 2026-10-31

The original all-tie result reflected **both limited sensitivity to small differences and a lack of consistent benefit from the first revision**. Reassessing the exact 24 original pairs with the more granular procedure, in both presentation orders, gives 3 stable preferences for the original, 3 for the first revision, 15 ties, and 3 order-sensitive results. A new rubric must not turn those balanced outcomes into an improvement claim.

## Evaluator checks

Both old and new procedures pass all 12 presentations of six constructed control pairs: identical answers, visible context/factual damage, and injected explanatory or repetitive wording. These are construct controls, not participant preference labels.

A separate check uses eight fixed pairs from [USR's original human annotations](https://shikib.com/usr), with scores and model labels hidden. On four large differences in mean human Overall rating, both procedures prefer the higher-rated response in both orders (4/4 pairs). On four small, overlapping-rating differences, the old procedure has two stable preferences and two ties; the new procedure has two stable preferences and two order-sensitive decisions. **This does not establish that the new evaluator is better aligned with people.** Mean ratings are not direct pairwise labels, and the small gaps are uncertain.

The new procedure records specific conversational costs, allows slight preferences, and swaps the candidate order. A result is counted as stable only when both orders agree; disagreements, including preference/tie disagreements, are reported separately. Assessment remains same-family, model-based evidence. Runs span October 30–31 after interrupted network availability; the six fresh final comparisons interleave the two versions in the same later batch. A model name is not a guarantee of an immutable backend checkpoint.

## What people actually said

Source windows come from recorded [Switchboard/SwDA telephone conversations](https://convokit.cornell.edu/documentation/switchboard.html) and human-written [EmpatheticDialogues](https://aclanthology.org/P19-1534/). The latter are elicited crowdworker dialogues, not recordings of spontaneous everyday interaction. Twenty-four windows were used: six development, twelve iterated comparison, and six fresh final checks. Human next responses were withheld during AI generation. The source IDs, normalization, selection procedure, and hashes are in [the manifest](human-comparison-manifest.json).

All sources are English. Only transcription markup is removed from the selected Switchboard text; verbal disfluencies remain. The EmpatheticDialogues comma token is decoded. Its CSV contains optional ninth-column response candidates and a few missing optional columns; only the verified first six conversation fields are used. No rows are silently skipped, and candidate responses are not treated as actual turns.

The models receive the same quoted prefix, but the recorded humans had wider conversational history, topic instructions, and personal knowledge that a short transcription window may omit. Accordingly, human continuations are observations rather than an information-matched gold standard. Some human responses introduce unsupported assumptions. Raw corpora are not bundled into the MIT-licensed skill.

## Skill iterations and frozen tests

- **v1:** original commit `689f6c9`.
- **v2:** first research revision, commit `81a0cc3`.
- **v3:** reduce repetitive reflection and interview-like prompts; stay with the live detail.
- **v4:** permit partial contributions, provisional readings, and small repairs following the user's emphasis on human imperfection.
- **v5 (final):** illustrate a complete reaction without a follow-up; avoid incidental factual-correction detours without endorsing a false premise.

The final skill SHA-256 is `67eafffbb796bb4a78344c6b75ed60dc9069692fbe4c4d3953b663a59ac406f2`. Each snapshot and the rubric were frozen before its associated generation. The twelve comparison contexts were inspected during iteration and are not independent final validation. After v5 was frozen, six fresh source windows were automatically selected from new Switchboard conversations and the EmpatheticDialogues test split, without inspecting their contents before generation. This is still a small convenience sample; public-data training contamination cannot be excluded.

## Observable differences and remaining gaps

| human     |        18 |                   11 |                       8   |
| v2        |        18 |                   17 |                      18.5 |
| v5        |        18 |                   15 |                      11.5 |

These are descriptive counts in eighteen matched contexts, using a question mark and whitespace words, not a human-likeness score. Final replies are shorter in this sample and ask slightly fewer questions, but still ask more often than the recorded continuations. Brevity and question frequency are not targets or quotas. The final instructions are longer than v2; a prompt-cost advantage has not been demonstrated.

In development, a light mention of a pet prompted v1/v2 to ask what always makes the person smile; the observed human asked the pet's name, and v3 asked the name too. A five-year craft project prompted broad challenge/origin questions in earlier versions; later versions asked about progress. A cheerful statement can now end with a wish rather than another question. These are examples, not reference answers copied into the skill. The two illustrative stopping-point examples in the skill were invented independently of corpus excerpts.

The assessor often prefers the AI continuation to the recorded human one. That must **not** be interpreted as outperforming humans, a Turing-test result, or validation of human imitation: the rubric favors certain helpful, specific continuations; source responses vary in quality; windows omit shared information; and same-family preference bias remains possible.

## Human imperfection and evidence boundaries

[Good-enough comprehension](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/current-directions/1467-8721.00158/), [incremental turn planning](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2015.00731/full), and [CReST self-repair findings](https://aclanthology.org/C16-1317/) motivate provisional, incomplete, jointly repaired conversation. They do not establish that this prompt reconstructs a human mind or that inserting mistakes improves interaction. The skill retains honest facts and explicit task completion while allowing a conversational turn to leave room for another person.

## Execution and regression checks

Generation and assessment use `gpt-6.1-sol`, medium reasoning, in separate ephemeral Codex calls with matched common prompts and tool use forbidden and checked. There are 126 completed corpus-based AI continuations across development and intermediate/final versions, plus 16 completed replies in two regression passes: **142 new generated replies**. Assessment calls are separate and are not counted as conversation replies. Original records and frozen hashes are retained locally.

Four intermediate assessment jobs and five v4 generation jobs encountered transient routing/network errors or a wall-clock timeout; their failed attempts were archived, excluded from scores, and retried from unchanged prompts. All planned final records completed. No observed tool events occurred in completed generation or assessment records.

The final six synthetic regression dialogues cover Korean listening, good news, humor and evidence-based updating, plus honest experience/memory and a detailed English explanation. All six pass the model audit and manual spot review. The cost example was independently recomputed with pandas: $45,000 versus $43,000 over three years. These checks support preservation of the tested behaviors, not Korean human-likeness validation.

No new human participants, live-user satisfaction study, independent model family, or statistical significance test was used. The practical conclusion is a more explicit, empirically inspected revision with modest local evidence and clearly unresolved generalization.
